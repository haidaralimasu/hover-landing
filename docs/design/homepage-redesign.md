## Applied design

Removed the What is Hover section. Replaced the old sequence with a split hero and illustrative product preview, USD/EUR/GBP accounts, three setup steps, a payment request example, transfer benefits, security, FAQs and a final download section.

Poppins, white backgrounds, grey surfaces, 20px cards and lime primary actions follow the requested brand direction. Official store badge artwork and destinations remain in place, with equal boxes for both stores. Product previews use illustrative sample amounts and are labelled as examples. No competitor ratings, customer counts or regulatory claims were copied.

The hero wording follows the owner's requested instant-receipt message. The accounts section explains that verification, availability and bank transfer timing depend on the account and payment method.
