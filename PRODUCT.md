# Hover — Product Context

## What it is
Marketing / landing site for **Hover**: a seedless, ZK-proof-authorized Solana
smart wallet. Core promise: **send money across borders in seconds** — sign in,
choose an amount, money is on its way. No seed phrase, no crypto ceremony.

## Register
**brand** — this site IS the product surface. Design is the pitch. Craft,
motion, and typographic confidence carry it; not utilitarian density.

## Platform
web (Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind v4)

## Audience
People sending money across borders who are tired of slow, expensive, seed-phrase
crypto. Non-technical first, crypto-native second. They need to feel *safe* and
*fast*, not lectured about ZK proofs.

## Voice
Plain, confident, calm. Short declaratives. "Send money across borders in
seconds." Never hypey, never jargon-first. Security is stated as fact, not sold.

## Design north star (source of truth: mobile app tokens)
Fully **monochrome, LIGHT** — white surfaces, black `#0A0A0A` ink, gray ramp, and
a dark graphite gradient for the "H" mark and primary button fill (black pill,
white label — matches the app's `Button.primary`). The app is light-themed
(`userInterfaceStyle: "light"`). **No hue anywhere** except real third-party brand
logos (Apple, Google, Solana). Font: **Poppins** (400/500/600/700/800). The
website must read as the same brand as the app.

## Anti-references (do NOT become these)
- Generic SaaS purple-gradient template.
- Fintech navy-and-gold.
- Crypto-neon / glassmorphism-everywhere.
- Gradient text, tracked uppercase eyebrows on every section, 01/02/03 scaffolding.
